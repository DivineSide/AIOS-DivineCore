# icps/

ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) definitions and discovery materials per vertical DivineSide has targeted.

## Status

| Folder | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [`coaching/`](coaching/) | **ACTIVE** (since June 2026) | Exam-prep & coaching institutes. Entry offer: AI question paper generation. See [`coaching/overview.md`](coaching/overview.md). |
| [`ecommerce-archived/`](ecommerce-archived/) | ARCHIVED | Pre-pivot UK DTC ecom / consumer health & beauty direction. RM 365 was anchor case study. Preserved as historical reference; do not load for current work. |

## What lives here

- `<vertical>/overview.md` — full ICP profile per active vertical: who they are, profile, geography, market size, entry offer, competition.
- `<vertical>/<sub-materials>` — discovery findings, sample proposals, prospect lists. (Operational scrape/qualify configs for outreach live separately in `divinecore-v2/worker/sales_os/Clients/<client>/icps/` — that's the runtime location.)

## What does NOT live here

- Working sales-OS scrape filters and lead pipelines (those live in `divinecore-v2/worker/sales_os/Clients/`).
- The strategic narrative for the whole agency (that's [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) §2 and the AIOS vision in §2b).
- Live offer mechanics (those live in [`../shared/context/sales-and-delivery/offer.md`](../shared/context/sales-and-delivery/offer.md)).

## When you add a new ICP

1. Create `icps/<vertical>/overview.md` mirroring `coaching/overview.md` structure.
2. Create `icps/<vertical>/.abstract.md` — one line, what's in the folder.
3. Update this file's status table with the new vertical's row.

## When you sunset an ICP

1. Rename to `<vertical>-archived/`.
2. Add `ARCHIVED.md` explaining the pivot date, reason, and pointer to the active ICP.
3. Update the `.abstract.md` to say "ARCHIVED" up front.
4. Update this file's status table.
