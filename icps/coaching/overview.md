## Who they are

**Owner-operators running exam-prep and coaching institutes.** Institutes that teach students toward exams, certifications, or skill outcomes — JEE/NEET coaching, SAT/ACT prep, UPSC/SSC coaching, professional certification training, language coaching, skill-based tutoring.

### Profile

- **Owner-operator.** Founder, principal, director — same person. May have started as a teacher themselves. India: 89% of the 68,181 coaching institutes are single-owner operations.
- **1–50 staff.** Lean. Wears multiple hats.
- **Multi-format teaching** — live classroom, recorded video, live calls, ebooks, study material, mock papers.
- **4–7 disconnected tools** — usually some combination of Classplus / Teachmint / Proctur / Meritto / GoHighLevel / WhatsApp / Google Sheets / a custom app. None of them talk to each other.
- **Already pays for software.** Software-budget habit is established (Classplus, Teachmint, Meritto, Proctur all have paying customers).
- **Most time-consuming activity:** content creation (question papers, presentations, study material). Named unprompted by the first real coaching owner conversation.
- **AI trust issues.** Won over by proof, not pitch.

## What they are NOT

- **School-age student platforms (B2C)** — that's a different model (Byju's, etc.).
- **Universities or colleges (higher education)** — different scale, different sales cycle. That's Risely AI's market.
- **E-learning content companies selling courses to consumers.**
- **Enterprise L&D buyers.**

## Why this vertical is structurally right for AIOS

In a coaching institute, one doubt asked by a student is simultaneously a teaching-quality signal, a curriculum gap indicator, a student-profile data point, a predictor of exam performance, and a reusable pattern across future students. **The information IS the product.** Every operational action becomes a data point the closed loop can learn from.

Compare:

| Business type | Data quality | Compounds over time? | AIOS fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical product | Transactions, inventory. What moved, not why. | No | Weak |
| Service business | Appointments, invoices. Core expertise stays in people's heads. | Barely | Moderate |
| SaaS | Rich usage data — but buyers build their own intelligence layers. | Yes — wrong buyer | Wrong market |
| **Information business** | **Every interaction is structured, reusable, causal context.** | **Yes — deeply** | **Best fit** |

After 6 months of operation, AIOS knows more about how to teach that institute's students than any generic platform. That accumulated intelligence is the moat.

## The entry offer

**AI-powered question paper generation.** Named unprompted by the first coaching owner as the single most time-consuming activity in his week. See [`../../shared/context/sales-and-delivery/offer.md`](../../shared/context/sales-and-delivery/offer.md) for the live offer.

**Why this specifically:**
- Highest pain (multiple sets per week, hours per set).
- Zero switching cost — doesn't touch their existing app.
- Tangible output from session one.
- Builds the content context layer (syllabus, format, difficulty calibration) that every future workflow needs.

## The four business functions, in priority order

1. **Product (education delivery)** — question papers, presentations, study material. **Entry point.** Highest pain, fragmented competition.
2. **Marketing** — result announcements, testimonials, social proof. Zero existing competition, immediate visible output.
3. **Customer service** — parent/student communication, doubt queue, fee follow-up. Currently 5+ hours/week of repetitive work.
4. **Sales** — batch recommendation, post-enrolment onboarding. **Most defended** (Meritto, GoHighLevel). Enter last.

## The discovery question (Pang runs every conversation)

> *"What would you have to give up or change about how you currently work to use something new?"*

Maps the hidden costs of switching, so we engineer them out of the product before they become objections.

## Distribution into this ICP

- **Warm intros first.** The first coaching contact has 50+ education-business connections and is more motivated to share us than we are to sell. Fastest path to clients #2–#5.
- **Pang's direct outreach.** India warm-list first. US/UK research in parallel — identifying communities, LinkedIn groups, subreddits.
- **Content engine** (YouTube AIOS series, LinkedIn brand presence) compounds underneath.
