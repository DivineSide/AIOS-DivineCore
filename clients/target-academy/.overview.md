## Conventions / what NOT to put here

- **Repo is PUBLIC.** Never commit the client's name, contact info, his materials, or anything derived from his content. `brain/` files stay anonymized ("the owner", "the institute") — same discipline as the discovery note.
- **Nothing leaves `review/` for `outputs/` without human QC.** Every correction gets a line in `brain/decisions.md`.
- **Grounded generation only.** `generate.py` draws facts from `corpus/` with sources cited; the LLM organizes and formats, it does not free-generate exam facts.
- No pricing numbers, no AIOS language in anything client-facing.
- Credentials in `.env` only (needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`).
