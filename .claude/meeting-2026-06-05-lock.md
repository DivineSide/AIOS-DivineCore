## Context (why we're switching, so we're aligned)

- **6 discovery calls.** The number-one pain that surfaced was ads and content. That is the one thing we cannot credibly build (no ads experience, brutal competition, volatile results).
- **Thin margins + tool fatigue.** These brands run on 14-15 tools already, so both the budget and the attention for us is low.
- **Time.** Building a beauty system from scratch is 3 to 6 months of figuring-out and R&D we do not have. The new niche has a proven playbook we can copy.
- **We are NOT abandoning the model.** The bucket-with-holes managed service still stands. We are pointing the same model at a niche where the workflows are already proven and we can actually build them.

## LOCK 1 — The niche (and what we sell)

### The candidates

| | Med spas | Home services |
|---|---|---|
| **Who pays** | High-ticket procedures, real margins | Price-sensitive, cheap buyers |
| **Competition** | Crowded with specialist tools (Zenoti, Pabau, AestheticsPro) | Most saturated AAA niche of all |
| **Build fit** | High, but booking/EMR integration adds work | High |
| **Reachability** | Mid, strong via multi-location referral flywheel | Cold call heavy |
| **Verdict** | **Best buyer.** Win with the full stack + niche depth | Volume play, worst margins |

*(Agencies are a validation shortcut to book calls fast, not the niche itself.)*

### Recommendation: US med spas, multi-location / franchise owners

- Best buyer, and the 2026 market is shifting to retention-driven growth, which is exactly our stack.
- Distribution via the **franchise referral flywheel:** land one multi-location owner, over-deliver, get referred across their network for free.

### What we sell: the 4-worker "department," not one tool

One automation is a freelancer they can fire and replace. A department is not. We sell:

1. **Database reactivation** ← we LEAD with this. Fastest cash, mines their existing patient list, zero ad spend, fastest proof.
2. **Reviews + referrals** (drives Google ranking and free leads)
3. **Website lead nurture** (5-minute follow-up on form fills)
4. **AI receptionist / missed-call textback**

We skip the 5th worker, paid ads, the one we cannot do well.
