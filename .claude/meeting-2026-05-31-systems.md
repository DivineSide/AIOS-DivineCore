## What a "system" actually is
A KPI is a target. A system is how you hit it, every time, even on a bad day.

**A system = 4 things, written down:**
1. **Target** — the number you commit to (per day / per week)
2. **Playbook** — the exact steps you follow to hit it
3. **Tracking** — where the number lives so you know if you hit it
4. **Who you're following** — someone who already did this. You don't invent it.

Anything less is intention, not a system.
