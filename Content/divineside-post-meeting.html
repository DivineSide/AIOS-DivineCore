2. The Core Offer — What We Build First

AI-powered question paper generation for coaching institutes.
The single most time-consuming activity, named unprompted, with a clear volume unlock. Zero competing tools that do this with institute-specific context. Fast to build, fast to demonstrate, fast to charge for.

The offer in plain language: "We build you an AI system that generates formatted, syllabus-accurate question papers in minutes — multiple sets, multiple difficulty levels, ready to use. What takes you hours today takes minutes. You can double your output without hiring anyone."

Why question papers specifically

  • Named as the most time-consuming task — highest pain, clearest ROI
  • Multiple sets per week = recurring value, not a one-time fix
  • Output is tangible and immediate — owner can see the result in the first session
  • No switching cost — doesn't touch their existing app at all
  • Builds the content context layer (syllabus, format, difficulty calibration) that powers every future workflow

What we are NOT building yet

  • PPT generation — second workflow, after question papers are proven
  • Result announcement engine — third workflow
  • Dropout detection — not relevant for adult learners
  • Notes generation — not valued by this ICP

3. The Switching Cost Question

Every product faces two types of cost from the customer's perspective:

Cost typeWhat it isIn our context
Financial cost The money they pay Our pricing — to be determined after first build
Hidden cost Extra effort, sacrifices, disruption, learning curve, things they have to give up Leaving their existing app, re-entering data, learning a new interface, losing their saved contacts and history, time spent onboarding

For question paper generation specifically — there is almost no switching cost. We don't ask them to leave their app. We don't touch their student data. We take a syllabus document and a format preference, generate papers, deliver as a PDF or Google Doc. They use it in whatever workflow they already have. Zero disruption.

The question Pang should investigate in every outreach conversation:
"What would you have to give up or change about how you currently work to use a tool like this?" — The answer tells you exactly what the real hidden cost is for each customer type. When we know the hidden costs, we engineer them out of the product before they become objections.
