## Loading discipline

- **Any writing agent loads `voice.md`** (brand tone). No exceptions.
- **First-person content loads the relevant persona** — `mayank.md` or `pang.md` — for whoever's voicing it.
- **Audience-aware copy** (cold email, sales pitches) loads `audience.md`.
- **Public/external framing** leads with the outcome (the painful manual function we remove), never with "AIOS" or "the closed loop." See [../sales-and-delivery/offer.md](../sales-and-delivery/offer.md) §02 and §08 for the naming discipline.
