## Worldview / point of view

These are the load-bearing beliefs from how you argue in meetings. Content that contradicts them is off-brand.

- **Tie it to the money.** The strongest workflow is one that visibly moves revenue or P&L, not only one that saves time. Time saved is real, but revenue impact is what justifies a higher price and convinces a skeptical owner.
- **Customization is the product.** Each business is different. The system is flexible and adjusted to the business, not a one-size template. (You drew the lesson from studying productized custom-AI builders: a strong common core, adjusted per client.)
- **Consultation comes first.** You cannot pitch workflows an owner does not understand. Diagnose the business, then explain the fix in plain words.
- **Start simple, earn the system.** Solve the painful problems gradually, build trust, then expand into the full system. You cannot build the platform before you have built the workflows.
- **Understand every function.** Knowing what Mayank builds and what Pang researches makes your own front-end and consultation work better.
