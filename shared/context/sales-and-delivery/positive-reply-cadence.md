# Positive-Reply → Booked-Call Cadence (LOCKED 2026-06-10)

> **The process for what to do every time a prospect replies positively, until the call is booked.** Built from the speed-to-lead data (5-min reply = 21x more likely to qualify) + Lee Hinkin's cold-reply-to-meeting method. Companion to the copy in [`../playbooks/reviews-offer-outreach.md`](../playbooks/reviews-offer-outreach.md) (LinkedIn / email / Loom templates) and [`../playbooks/cold-call-script.md`](../playbooks/cold-call-script.md) (the cold version of the call).
>
> Voice: casual, plain, no em-dashes. Placeholders: `[name]`, `[center]`, `[time]`, `[Loom link]`, `[link]`.

---

## The three principles (don't break these)

1. **Speed wins. Reply within 5 minutes.** 78% of buyers go with whoever responds first, and replying inside 5 minutes makes you 21x more likely to qualify them. Treat a positive reply like an alarm going off. Never apologize for speed, and never make a warm lead wait days for a promised asset (that is what kills them).
2. **Own the booking. Never hand off to a raw link.** Agree the time manually in the conversation (propose 2 specific slots in their timezone), then YOU add it to Calendly and send the invite. The link is backup, not the ask. A link makes them do the work, and warm leads cool while doing homework.
3. **Follow up more than feels comfortable.** Most people quit after 1-2 touches and then blame lead quality. These prospects already raised their hand. Run the full cadence below before you ever call a lead dead.

---

## Step 1 — classify the reply the moment it lands

| Type | Looks like | Goal |
|------|-----------|------|
| 🔥 **HOT** | "call me", "let's talk", "send me a time", "very interested" | Book within 24h. Compress the cadence. |
| 🌤️ **WARM** | "tell me more", "what's the cost", "interested but...", "send info" | Give value first (Loom / mini-audit), build a little trust, then book. Slightly longer runway. |

Address their **specific** query first (if they asked for pricing, answer pricing, then pivot to the call). Don't ignore what they actually said and just push for a meeting.

---

## Step 2 — the cadence (Day 0 = their positive reply)

Daily touches for the first week. No 2-day gaps, warm leads cool fast.

| Day | Channel | Action |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **0** (within 5 min) | Email reply **+ LinkedIn connection request** | Answer their query. **HOT:** propose 2 times + offer to call now. **WARM:** send the value asset (Loom/audit) + soft 2-time ask. Send the LinkedIn connect immediately. |
| **+1** | Email | No reply → 2-line bump, re-offer 2 fresh times. |
| **+2** | Warm call (if you have the number) **or** LinkedIn DM | Switch channel. Call if you can, that's the highest-converting move. |
| **+3** | **Warm call** + voicemail + text | Primary call push. Calling is what almost nobody does and it converts best. |
| **+4** | Email (value) | Send something genuinely useful (a competitor teardown, mini-audit) + re-ask. |
| **+5** | LinkedIn or email | One more light angle. |
| **+7** | Email (break-up) | "Should I close this out?" These get replies. |
| **+14, +30, then monthly** | Light nurture | Stay on the radar. A no now is not a no forever. |

**When to call:** HOT lead with a number → Day 0, immediately. Everyone else → Day +2 to +3, once the first email touches haven't converted. Don't open with a cold phone call on someone who only accepted a LinkedIn connection and never gave a number.

## Step 3 — the messages

### Touch 0 — HOT reply
> Hi [name], great to hear from you and thanks for the quick reply. [One line answering their exact question.] Let's get a time locked in. Does [Thu 2pm] or [Fri 11am] your time work? I'll send the invite straight over. And if it's easier, I'm happy to just call you today, whatever suits you.

### Touch 0 — WARM reply
> Hi [name], thanks so much for getting back to me. [One line answering their query.] I made you a quick 90-second video showing exactly what I'd do for you: [Loom link]. Have a watch when you get a sec. If it looks useful, does [Thu 2pm] or [Fri 11am] your time work for a quick 15-min call? I'll send the invite over.

### Touch +1 — email bump
> Hi [name], just floating this back up. Would [Tue 3pm] or [Wed 10am] your time work for a quick call? Happy to work around you.

### Touch +3 — LinkedIn message
> Hey [name], sent you a couple of notes about getting reviews flowing for [center], not sure they're landing in your inbox. Easier to just grab 15 min for a quick call? What works this week?

### Touch +5 — warm call (mini-script)
> "Hi, is this [name]? Hey [name], it's Pang, I emailed you about the review system for [center]. Caught you at an okay time for two minutes?"
> [yes]
> "Awesome, I'll be quick. You'd mentioned you were interested, so I figured a quick call beats ten emails back and forth. [Re-state the value in one line.] I'd love to show you exactly what it'd look like for you, takes about 15 minutes. Does tomorrow at 2 or is 4 better?"
> [book it, get their email, send the invite while on the call]

**Voicemail (under 20s):**
> "Hey [name], it's Pang, I emailed you about getting [center] more Google reviews. Honestly easier to chat for two minutes than go back and forth on email. I'll try you again, or grab me at [number]. Talk soon."

### Touch +8 — value re-engage
> Hi [name], was looking at [competitor] near you and noticed [specific thing]. [One useful insight.] Reminded me I still owe you that walkthrough. Want me to send a couple of times for a quick call?

### Touch +12 — break-up
> Hi [name], I don't want to keep cluttering your inbox. Should I close this out for now, or is getting more reviews still something you want to sort this quarter? Either answer is totally fine.

## Step 4 — booking mechanic

1. Agree the exact time **in the conversation** (propose 2 slots, they pick).
2. YOU add it to Calendly and send the calendar invite. Don't make them self-book.
3. Calendly runs the automated email + SMS reminders, which is what cuts no-shows.

## Step 5 — show-up sequence (after it's booked)

- **On booking:** calendar invite + one-line confirmation.
- **Morning of:** "Hey [name], looking forward to [time]. Took a quick look at a couple of your competitors, found something interesting to show you."
- **3 hours before:** "Sent the Zoom link to your email, let me know you got it."
- **10 min before:** "Here's the link: [link]. See you in 10."

## Step 6 — track everything

One place, every prospect, or they fall through cracks. HubSpot free tier or a simple Trello/sheet. Columns: Name · Source · Temp (hot/warm) · Last touch · Next touch date · Stage (Replied → Booked → Showed → Closed → Dead). Filter by "next touch date = today" each morning.
