# Reviews + Referrals Offer — Outreach Templates (copy-paste)

> **Ready-to-use copy for Pang's US reviews/referrals offer, 2026-06-10.** The concrete, send-it-now versions of the warm-reply LinkedIn message, the Loom-delivery email, the 90-second Loom script, and the Notion contrast visual. Companion to the cold-call version in [`cold-call-script.md`](cold-call-script.md) and the engage-first LinkedIn sequence in [`linkedin-playbook.md`](linkedin-playbook.md) §14.
>
> **Rules baked in:** no fabricated case study (the two-week free trial is the de-risk), no incentivized Google reviews (no gift card / raffle / charity tied to leaving a public review), lead with value not the ask, no over-apologizing in delivery copy. Voice: casual, plain, no em-dashes. Placeholders: `[name]`, `[center]`, `[year]`, `[Loom link]`.

## 1. LinkedIn first message (connection accepted, relationship-first, NO ask)

Use when an ICP prospect accepts the connection. Per `linkedin-playbook.md` §14, the first message is relationship only. The ask goes in message two once they reply.

> Hey [name], thanks for connecting. You probably saw my email. Honestly, given your background you're exactly the kind of person I want to learn from, so I'm really glad you accepted. How's everything going with the coaching business at the moment? Also went through a few of your posts and genuinely enjoyed them, I'll be keeping an eye out and supporting what you put out.

**Message two (after they reply), soft ask:**
> Would love to pick your brain for 15 min on what I'm building, the $50 for your time stands either way. This week work?

## 2. Loom-delivery email (reply in the existing thread, no new subject)

Short and confident. The Loom carries the pitch, so the email's only job is to get them to press play. Do not apologize or lead with your own schedule.

> Hi [name], thanks for getting back to me, really appreciate it. I made you a quick 90-second video, I pulled up your Google profile and walked through exactly what I'd do to get you reviews and move you up the rankings. [Loom link] Give it a watch when you get a sec, and if it looks useful just reply here and I'll send over a booking link. Talk soon, Pang

## 3. Loom script (~90 seconds)

Open with a 5-second hello, go straight to their Google profile, lead with value, no upfront offer dump, soft referral tease only. Swap the AI conversation example to match their world (the one below is for a college admissions consultant).

**[Opener, ~5s, on camera]**
> "Hey [name], Pang here, thanks for the reply."

**[Share screen on their Google profile, ~25s]**
> "So this is you on Google. You've been going about [year]s, and given your background as a Harvard admissions officer, you've clearly helped a lot of students get where they wanted. But you've got zero reviews, and your profile isn't really set up, no photo, no posts, the info's incomplete. My mission is simple: get you reviews and get you to the top when a parent searches for an admissions consultant."

**[~35s]**
> "So here's what we do. It's a two-week free trial. We reach out to your past clients, but not with those dead templated bot texts, 'Hi, thanks for choosing us, please leave a review,' nobody replies to those. We use AI that actually has a conversation. It opens with something real, like 'Hi Sarah, how's Emma settling in at college? You must be so proud,' waits for the reply, talks like a real person, and works its way naturally around to asking for the review."

**[~15s]**
> "And it keeps running. Every time you finish with a new client, the AI follows up on its own, so you get a steady stream of reviews without doing anything. We can even plug a referral system into it later, but I'll save that for the call."

**[Close, ~12s]**
> "Is this worth a quick 15 minutes? I'd genuinely love to answer your questions and show you more, I'm excited to help. Just reply to this email and I'll send my booking link. Talk soon."

**Recording tips:** have their Google profile + two competitor profiles open in tabs before recording, keep the face-cam bubble on, one take is fine, scroll slowly so the visuals track what you're saying.

## 4. Notion contrast visual (share on screen during the Loom)

Template-bot at the top (makes them wince), real AI conversation below (makes them go "oh, that's nice"). Scroll slowly from top to bottom while narrating.

### ❌ What most review tools send

> **To: Sarah**
> Hi Sarah, thanks for choosing [Center]! If you have 30 seconds, we'd really appreciate a 5-star Google review. Click here: [link]

One message. Generic. Feels like a bot. Most people ignore it.

### ✅ What we actually do

> **You:** Hi Sarah, how's Emma settling in at college? You must be so proud of her.
> **Sarah:** She's loving it! Thank you so much, we couldn't have done it without you.
> **You:** That genuinely makes my day. She worked so hard for it. Quick thing, would you mind sharing a couple of words about your experience? It helps other families going through the same stress find us. I'll send the link, takes a minute.
> **Sarah:** Of course, happy to. Send it over.
> **You:** You're a star, thank you Sarah. Here it is: [link]. And give Emma my best.

The AI reads each reply and responds to it, so every conversation is different and every one sounds like you.

## Reuse notes

- The Loom script and AI example are written for an admissions consultant. For a test-prep / tutoring center, swap the example to a student/score milestone ("how's [child] getting on, how did the SATs go?").
- If a prospect asks for proof or a past client, do NOT invent one. Use: "you'd be one of my first in this niche, that's why the free trial, the risk is on me." Once the first free-trial client gets a real result, add a true result line here.
- Compliance: never tie a gift card, raffle, or donation to leaving a public Google review (Google can remove reviews / suspend the profile). Incentivize feedback/responses if needed, never the public post.
