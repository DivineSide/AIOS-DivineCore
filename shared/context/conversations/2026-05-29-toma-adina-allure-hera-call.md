She's getting likes and views but no buyers from cold traffic. **Word-of-mouth offline is her real growth engine, not paid ads.**

## The actual lessons (this is the most important section of this note)

This call is the **first real test of the locked-niche workflow hypothesis with a DTC operator who fits the niche** (Shopify, beauty, skincare, ships UK). Toma's verdict was decisive: the workflows we want to sell are mostly **already commodity infrastructure** in the DTC stack. Klaviyo and Judge.me ship them by default. ChatGPT helps founders set them up for free.

**Three concrete signals for the 2026-06-05 lock meeting:**

1. **The "everyone's already doing this" anxiety was partially real.** It does NOT apply to marketplace sellers (their tools are platform-native). It DOES apply to DTC operators who built their own store, even at small scale. Klaviyo's recent autonomous-marketing features specifically commoditize what we proposed. **The current £1k-£2.5k workflow build offer is at risk of being obviated by Klaviyo's own product roadmap.**

2. **The actual gap she identified is real and underserved: social media comment automation.** None of the major tools handle automated, voice-matched replies to Instagram and Facebook ad comments at the level she'd want. **This is one workflow worth keeping as a v1 menu item.**

3. **The real pain in DTC is NOT workflow automation. It's content quality and audience targeting.** Her words: *"To have the impact and to go to the right customers."* Toma's wand wish points away from our offer entirely and toward a different product category (creative quality, audience targeting, content strategy). This is the second time in three days that a real operator has pointed at content/creative as the actual pain. The aircon-cleaner friend also implicitly pointed there ("I'm trying to automate video editing"). **Mayank's Branding OS module may be where the real value lives, not the Sales OS workflow stack.**

4. **Pre-call website research has a blind spot.** The earlier Allure Hera website analysis I did concluded she had no reviews, no replenishment, no winback — because I couldn't see her backend Klaviyo flows from the public site. She has all of them. **Lesson for future research:** website front-end shows what's missing in the *display layer* but not the *automation layer*. Don't extrapolate from one to the other.

## Pivot options Pang surfaced on the call

Toma's feedback narrowed the offer to three viable angles:

1. **New Shopify stores** that haven't yet set up automations (greenfield, but smallest budget)
2. **Old Shopify stores** that never set up automations (likely older / less technical founders)
3. **Existing operators** who have flows running but want them optimized (sharper personalization, voice-matching, performance lift)

**Option 3 is the most defensible at the £1M-£5M ARR target.** Established brands have the flows but probably haven't tuned them — that's a real consulting/optimization play, not a workflow-building play. **Worth re-shaping the offer around "optimize what you have, don't build from scratch" before the lock meeting.**
