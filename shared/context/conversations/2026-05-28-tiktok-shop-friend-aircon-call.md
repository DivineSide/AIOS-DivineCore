## The actual lesson (offer-doc input, not niche signal)

This call is more useful for [offer.md](../sales-and-delivery/offer.md) and positioning than it is for niche validation. Specifically:

1. **Productize the system, don't sell workflows.** The friend independently arrived at the exact "stack 6-7 workflows into a sellable system" message that JP's path argues for. Confirms the destination state in offer.md §01-09 is the right direction, not the §00 "custom workflow build per engagement" current state. The §00 framing is correct for *learning* during the sprint but is exactly what the friend pushed back on as the wrong way to sell.
2. **Subscription pricing is what real operators expect for ongoing systems.** Reinforces the £350/mo retainer direction in offer.md §03. The "0% upfront, 100% on delivery" mechanic is interesting but doesn't address his deeper concern (accountability for what happens after delivery).
3. **Brand and accountability beat price.** Pang's freelancer-with-no-company positioning is the single biggest blocker right now. The "DivineSide" brand exists on paper but he wasn't operating as it on this call. Need to make the agency identity real and externalized.
4. **Risk framing > value framing** for established business owners. JP's "save 10 hours/week" framing (TYPE A guarantee) and "the system performs the functions" framing (TYPE B) both miss this. For a real operator with revenue at stake, the more important promise is **"we will not break your brand."** Worth a section in [guarantee.md](../sales-and-delivery/guarantee.md) — something about operational safety guarantees (rollback, monitoring, insurance/SLAs).
5. **Customer-designed solutions don't work.** The "of these 6 workflows, which would you want first?" question (in the JP-framework script) needs revision. Real operators don't know which to pick. Show them the system, name the ROI, let them say yes or no.

Worth flagging for the 2026-06-05 lock meeting as evidence that the **destination productized offer is the right shape**, even before pilots ship. Custom workflow builds are still the right vehicle for *learning the niche*, but they should not be how we *sell* once Phase 2 starts.
