## Context

Second call with Rahmatul. He works at Intrepit (Intrepid Asia), an end-to-end ecom enabler servicing APAC. They handle warehousing, ads, and platform management for clients on Shopee, Lazada, and Zalora. He has no purchasing authority. Pang ran the JP-framework approach (mentor frame, then pillar questions on reviews, customer database, replenishment, daily pain). Pure validation conversation. 20 min.

## Stack snapshot

- **Clients sell on marketplaces only:** Shopee (big in Indonesia), Lazada (big in Singapore), Zalora. No own-website DTC mentioned.
- **Intrepit handles full ecom stack for clients:** warehousing, ads, platform ops.
- **AI tooling in use:** Shopee native AI chatbot (the main one Rahmatul referenced).
- **Customer data ownership:** brands generally do NOT have their own customer database. Customer relationship sits with the marketplace.

## The 8 pillars: what's set up, what's not

- **Reviews:** Marketplace-native templates already exist. Shopee has AI-templated review request flow built in. Personalization at the seller level is limited.
- **Referrals:** Not discussed.
- **Lapsed winback:** **Gap.** Brands lack a customer database, so winback to old customers isn't happening. Rahmatul flagged this as a real opportunity but tied it to the same "we don't own the customer" problem.
- **Replenishment:** **Already solved by platform.** Shopee/Lazada offer 7/30/60-day reorder reminder templates natively. Usage is capped (50-200 chats), so value to a third-party builder is limited.
- **Ads / creative:** Handled by the enabler. The actual pain is not creative quality, it's **platform instability** (see "Surprise quote" below).
- **Inquiry response speed:** Not directly discussed, but Shopee AI chatbot covers this poorly (50% relevant).
- **UGC pipeline:** Not discussed.
- **Current AI usage:** Shopee native AI chatbot. Rahmatul rates it at ~50% relevance. Misses or mishandles half of customer questions, especially when questions are poorly typed.

## Surprise / non-obvious quote

> *"The Shopee AI chatbot, I can say the 50% the answer is relevant, 50% is not relevant or is not answering any of the questions of the customers."*

And on the #1 pain across all platforms:

> *"Today, they launched ABC features, and then the next day, they changed it really instantly and quick. So that's the kind of situation that we have to adapt and encourage instant, like instant, quick changes on the platform."*

## Wand wish

Not asked verbatim, but the implicit answer was clear: **stability across marketplace platforms.** When asked the most troublesome part of his daily work, he said platform instability (Shopee/Lazada changing features without notice) is the #1 operational pain. He didn't name a workflow he wished would automate; he named a structural problem (platform volatility) that no third-party AI can solve.

## Niche fit verdict

**NO.** Intrepit's clients are 100% marketplace-side (Shopee, Lazada, Zalora). Per the locked exclusion in [.claude/alignment-2026-05-22.md](../../../.claude/alignment-2026-05-22.md) and [CLAUDE.md §2](../../../CLAUDE.md), marketplace sellers are out of scope. The structural reason came out of this call directly: marketplace sellers don't own the customer, and the platform provides native versions of every workflow we'd sell (reviews, replenishment, broadcast chat, AI chatbot). What's left for third-party tools is squeezed into the gaps the platform doesn't cover. That's a different business than ours.
